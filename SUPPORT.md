# Support

- **Getting started:** the guides on [alles-automatisch.de](https://alles-automatisch.de/lernen/mealie-atelier) (German) and the READMEs of [Mealie Atelier Cards](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/mealie-atelier-cards) and [Mealie Atelier Bridge](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/mealie-atelier-bridge).
- **Common questions:** the [Cards FAQ](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/mealie-atelier-cards/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md) and the [Bridge FAQ](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/mealie-atelier-bridge/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md).
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/mealie-atelier/discussions).
- **Bugs:** an issue in the [Cards](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/mealie-atelier-cards/issues) or [Bridge](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/mealie-atelier-bridge/issues) repository.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** never in public, use the private reporting links in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

When reporting a problem, include the Cards and Bridge versions, your Home Assistant and Mealie versions, what you expected, what happened and how to reproduce it. Remove hostnames, tokens, personal recipes and other personal data from logs and screenshots.

If the project is useful to you, you can [support it on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/allesautomatisch) or [join the Alles Automatisch YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@alles-automatisch/join). Support is optional and does not unlock features.
