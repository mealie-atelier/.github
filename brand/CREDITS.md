# Brand asset credits

## Food imagery

- Organization and product icons plus both repository previews: original food photograph generated for Mealie Atelier, depicting handmade pasta with tomatoes and basil. The self-contained SVG masters embed this image so exports do not depend on relative asset loading.
- The former Unsplash pasta-making photograph remains in the folder as a legacy source asset; current icon and preview artwork no longer uses it.

## Export dimensions

- `mealie-atelier.png`: 1024 × 1024 pixel organization icon master.
- PWA exports: 192 × 192, 512 × 512 and 512 × 512 maskable.
- Apple touch icon: 180 × 180 pixels.
- Each product icon has 192 × 192, 512 × 512 and 1024 × 1024 pixel PNG exports.
- Each repository social preview is 1280 × 640 pixels.
