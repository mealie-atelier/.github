# Brand asset credits

## Typeface

- Wordmark and social preview text: [Bricolage Grotesque](https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage), SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font file and license are in `source/font/` (`BricolageGrotesque.ttf`, `OFL.txt`). The wordmark in the lockups is outlined to paths.

## Marks

- The Week Dial marks and lockups are original artwork for Mealie Atelier, generated by the scripts in `source/`. They contain no photographs or third-party artwork.

## Export dimensions

- Social previews: 1280 × 640 pixels (PNG and JPG).
- Avatar: 500 × 500 pixels. Apple touch icon: 180 × 180 pixels.
- Web app icons: 192 × 192, 512 × 512 and 512 × 512 maskable.
- Home Assistant brand set: icon 256 and 512 pixels, logo 128 and 256 pixels high.
- Favicons: SVG, 16 and 32 pixel PNG, and ICO with 16, 32 and 48 pixels.
